The story of how I ended up here.

My autobiography

I grew up on a ranch in Western Montana and went to college for diesel mechanics. After graduation, I realized I would rather be behind the wheel than under the hood.

I spent the first half of my adult life driving trucks until I achieved my dream: owning my own truck. After wrecking my first truck and struggling to keep the second one running, I found myself out of work. That is where my writing path began.

From the beginning, I knew I wanted to publish independently. That way, I maintain control and take responsibility for my work.

After completing my first published book, I wanted a website to help promote indie authors. So I created Indies First. It is still growing.

I had so much fun building Indies First that I wanted to do it again. So I built Freedom Almanac to make it easier for people to see what Congress is doing. Now, I have built my author website: Stories n Sites.

A note about my work

My writing. My editing. My formatting. My marketing. It is all me. I publish through Draft2Digital.

I use AI to help with the coding of my websites. The design, and testing are all me.
